Japan's networks, and why a CTSA hub

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
03
Japan has national hospital networks but no hub program. We came to see how one hub serves a whole state.
01
42 + 44
The 42 university hospitals form a council. It shares practice; it is not a hub.
02
NHO 140
NHO runs 140 hospitals with its own ARO and data center.
03
CCTSI
One hub serving universities, hospitals and communities statewide.

Japan's Core Clinical Research Hospitals

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
04
A legal status, not a grant. The Minister approves it; 16 hospitals hold it today.
01
Since 2015
Created in 2015. Approval and renewal follow published criteria.
02
A status
Unlike a CTSA award, a status the hospital keeps until it fails the criteria.
03
No office
No national program office sets goals. The criteria are the whole instrument.

What the criteria count

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
05
Three-year counts, in principle in-house, decide approval. Support to others is a floor, not a score.
01
8 trials
Drug-approval trials, or 4 plus 40 others. In principle led by the hospital's own PI.
02
45 papers
Peer-reviewed English papers. In principle the first author is from the hospital.
03
15 supports
A fixed floor. Beyond it, only full support counts: protocol, DM and monitoring.

What happens as a result

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
06
Counting in-house output creates an incentive that works against the hub function.
01
Small trials
Hospitals launch small, easy-to-start trials and multiply papers to meet the thresholds.
02
Penalty
Every person or dollar moved to supporting others lowers the hospital's own metrics.
03
Floor stays
The 2026 review keeps support as a floor; its effort was judged too hard to score.

Three shifts we propose

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
07
Re-weight, do not abolish. Each shift has a working US counterpart in how NIH funds hubs and functions.
01
Outcomes
From trial counts to approvals.
→
02
Support
Credit research done elsewhere.
→
03
Network
Core platform plus networks.
Measure what a hub enables, not what it keeps in-house.

Measured by our own yardstick

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
08
Apply the proposed axis to ourselves. None of this counts today; all of it enabled others' research.
01
Ptosh
EDC platform for academic trials; CDISC encoded at data entry.
02
NHO
CDISC adopted across the National Hospital Organization's multicenter trials.
03
aCRF.jp
Open annotated CRF library, and the JASPEHR group on EHR-to-research data.

What we came to ask

THE UNIVERSITY OF TOKYO HOSPITAL / CLINICAL RESEARCH PROMOTION CENTER
09
Thirteen written questions in seven themes; you have grouped the answers for 10:10. DCRI sharpened three.
01
Value
Which numbers NCATS reacts to; how partners' research is credited (A–C).
02
People
Support workforce and retention; at DCRI, culture and turnover under 6% (D).
03
System
How the hub relates to the AROs around it, and to data standards (E–G).

What we most want to learn

Review

If counted
If in-house counts decided hub status, how would CCTSI and its partners change? (Q3)
